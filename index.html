// CASE FILE — OBJECT 001

THE SHOE

Photographed under low light. Recovered from the long grass after the third match. Material analysis pending. Do not touch the sole.

// FIELD NOTES — RESTRICTED

LORE

FILE 0x14-MNTS

The following entries were recovered from a notebook left in section G of the locker room. Pages stained green. Handwriting unidentified. Reproduced without correction.

— EDITOR, KICKERSHOE

ENTRY 01WHO IS THE MANTIS?+

Born in the wet grass east of the river. Smaller than his peers. Quieter. He watched football from behind a leaf for nine seasons before he ever touched a ball. He is, by all accounts, still alive.

ENTRY 02THE BOOT'S FIRST MATCH+

A back-alley five-a-side, no name on the sheet. Player wore them straight from a paper bag. Scored four. Vanished at half-time. The bag was never returned.

ENTRY 03THE HIDDEN INSPIRATION+

A page torn from the same notebook shows slow-motion sketches of a free-kick stance — weight back, chest out, exactly 2.3 seconds of stillness before contact. Cross-referenced against known footage, the resemblance to a certain Portuguese forward is, we're told, "statistically improbable to be coincidental." Nobody involved will go on record. Nobody involved has denied it either.

ENTRY 04THE DESIGN BRIEF+

Nothing on this boot is decorative. The forward-raked ankle collar mirrors a mantis's raptorial forelimb — cocked, patient, built for one explosive strike rather than ninety patient minutes of chasing the game. The eight-stud sole plate spreads push-off force the way a mantis balances across four rear legs: silent until it isn't. The one flourish that isn't structural — the gold suture at the heel — exists purely because a certain someone has never worn a boot without a signature detail, and old habits are apparently contagious, even second-hand.

ENTRY 05WHY GREEN?+

Because invisibility is a kind of armour. Because the grass owes him nothing and everything. Because gold without green is just a bruise.

ENTRY 06THE GOLD STITCHING+

Filament is real. We tested it. We don't know where he gets it. He won't say.

ENTRY 07THE PITCH+

Internally logged as Project SIUUU STOMPER. Shopped, under a fake company name, to four major boot manufacturers. Two called the design "derivative." One asked, seriously, whether the mantis was trademarked. One went quiet for six weeks and came back asking for worldwide exclusive rights — at which point we stopped answering the phone, because by then we'd already decided: this one isn't for sale. If a certain someone in Riyadh or Turin is reading this, though — the mantis already built it for you. He doesn't do phone calls. Try leaving a leaf.
